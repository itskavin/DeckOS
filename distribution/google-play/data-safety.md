Google's [Data safety definitions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469?hl=en) include SDK handling and off-device ephemeral processing. Do not assume a TLS link automatically meets the Data safety end-to-end encryption exception. A developer-operated cloud is not required for data to leave the phone. Whether the user-operated companion counts as a recipient that changes final collection/sharing treatment requires an explicit owner assessment of the complete flow; do not guess “none” simply because the LAN server is local.

The QR plugin is `mobile_scanner` 7.2.0, resolving bundled `com.google.mlkit:barcode-scanning:17.3.0` rather than the Play Services download mode. Its metrics handling follows [ML Kit's Android disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure) and [privacy terms](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms). No Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, ad SDK, advertising-ID permission or Play Billing dependency was found in the app's declared dependencies or this merged manifest. ML Kit telemetry still exists.

## Proposed top-level answers

- **Contains ads:** No. **In-app purchases:** No. Do not invent a paid tier.
- **Creates DeckOS accounts:** No. Connecting an external provider or a local PC is not a DeckOS account sign-up flow. Do not claim an account-deletion URL or a hosted deletion service exists.
- **Collects/shares required user data types:** Yes, SDK diagnostics must be evaluated and disclosed. Complete per-type answers after the table is reconciled with the final SDK/transport build.
- **All user data encrypted in transit:** **Do not claim Yes yet.** Native phone-to-PC REST/WSS now use verified TLS and Android release denies cleartext. The same-PC browser configurator uses loopback HTTP, optional companion integrations can be configured with HTTP, and forthcoming billing adds separate flows. Assess the exact final product boundaries and configuration before answering.
- **Data deletion requests:** Local removal controls exist for device-owned data, but there is no developer-hosted data deletion endpoint or control over Google diagnostics. Use the actual choices shown by the form; do not promise deleting third-party data. Support contact is not proof of such an endpoint.
- **Independent security review / certifications:** None claimed.
- **App access:** Restricted by the PC companion requirement. Supply real setup/access instructions; do not declare every feature accessible unaided.
- **Target audience and IARC:** Owner must complete the actual questionnaire. PC automation tools are not presented as child-directed. Do not invent target ages or an “Everyone” rating on the owner's behalf. There are no implemented social feeds, gambling, health services, financial transactions, news or generative-AI chat functions in the Android remote. Optional user-configured shortcuts do not justify fabricated content declarations.

## Permissions and components to reconcile with final manifest

The release manifest, not this list, is authoritative. This merged manifest includes INTERNET, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE, CAMERA and the app's internal signature permission DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. Keeping the foreground screen awake uses window flags; there is no WAKE_LOCK permission in this build. Camera hardware is optional; QR scanning asks for permission at use. The image picker uses the Android/system selection flow; no broad storage/media-library permission appears in this merged manifest.

No Android foreground service, background location, accessibility service, notification listener, full-screen intent, device administration, SMS/contacts access, VPN, package installation, microphone or Play Billing feature is intended by this candidate. Do not add such permissions just to satisfy an imagined declaration. Reconcile this inventory with the generated manifest and actual SDK components before submission.
