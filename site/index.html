How it works

Set up in a minute.
Three steps, no sign-up.

  1. 1

    Install on your computer.

    Windows gets an installer that keeps itself up to date. On macOS and Linux it runs quietly in the background.

  2. 2

    Scan the QR code.

    Open DeckOS on your phone and point it at the code. It finds your computer on its own — or plug an Android phone in over USB.

  3. 3

    Tap.

    That's it. Build pages in the command center and every change lands on your phone instantly.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is DeckOS really free?

Yes. DeckOS is open source under the Apache-2.0 licence. There's no subscription, no paid tier and no limit on pages or buttons.

Which devices does it work with?

The deck runs on Android phones and tablets, iPhone and iPad. The companion app runs on Windows 10 (1809 or later, 64-bit), macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Linux (x64 and arm64). The macOS and Linux versions run as a background service and are newer than the Windows one.

Do my phone and computer need to be on the same Wi-Fi?

They need to be on the same local network — the phone finds the computer automatically, or you pair by scanning a QR code. Android phones can also connect over a USB cable, no Wi-Fi needed. Guest networks that isolate devices from each other will block the connection.

How do I use it on an iPhone or iPad?

Scan the pairing QR code from your computer and the deck opens right in Safari — nothing to install. A native iOS app is part of the same open-source project; see the docs for install options.

Does anything get sent to the internet?

Not by DeckOS. There's no account, no server and no telemetry. The only internet traffic comes from integrations you choose to connect, like Spotify or Twitch — and that goes straight from your computer to that service.

Can I bring my Elgato Stream Deck setup?

Yes. DeckOS imports Elgato Stream Deck profiles, so you don't have to rebuild your pages by hand.

Do the integrations need anything extra?

Some services have their own requirements: Spotify playback control needs Spotify Premium, and the soundboard plays into your mic through a virtual audio cable. The docs walk through each integration.

Windows says the installer is unrecognised. Is it safe?

SmartScreen warns about apps that aren't yet widely downloaded. Every release is built in the open by GitHub Actions from the public source code — you can check exactly what went into it.

How is DeckOS related to DroidDeck?

DeckOS is built on DroidDeck by ggfto, an open-source Android-to-Windows deck. DeckOS takes it to every phone and every desktop, with a redesigned app and command center.
